# Home page for an architecture studio

Okafor & Lind is an eleven-person architecture practice in Rotterdam that works mostly on schools, libraries and housing for public clients. The audience is a municipal client or competition jury deciding whether to shortlist them. The page should show the work first and sound like people who build things.

- Noorderhaven Boathouse — Groningen, 2022 — rowing club and public slipway
- Waalhaven Workshops — Rotterdam, 2021 — light-industrial units for 40 small firms
