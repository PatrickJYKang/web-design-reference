# Pricing page for a scheduling product

Halyard is scheduling software for small design and architecture studios: who is on which project, this week and next quarter. The audience is a studio owner or operations lead comparing it with a spreadsheet. The page should make the right plan obvious in under a minute.

## Content

Nav: Product · Customers · Pricing · Log in · Start free

Headline: Pay for the studio you have

Subhead: Every plan includes unlimited projects and unlimited viewers. You pay for the people you schedule.

Billing toggle: Monthly / Yearly (two months free)

Plans:

**Solo** — $0. For one person keeping their own calendar honest.
- 1 scheduled person
- Week and month views
- Calendar sync

**Studio** — $9 per scheduled person per month. For teams that plan together. Most studios choose this.
- Everything in Solo
- Capacity view across the whole team
- Time off and public holidays
- Project budgets in hours
- Slack and email digests

**Practice** — $16 per scheduled person per month. For studios with several offices or disciplines.
- Everything in Studio
- Multiple offices and time zones
- Roles and permissions
- Forecast against fees
- Priority support

Actions: Start free · Start 30-day trial · Talk to us

Questions:
- **Who counts as a scheduled person?** Anyone you assign to projects. Clients, freelancers you only share plans with, and people who just look are free.
- **Can we switch plans mid-month?** Yes. We charge or credit the difference for the days left.
- **Do you offer a discount for non-profits and schools?** Half price on any plan. Write to us from your organisation's address.
- **What happens to our data if we leave?** Export everything as CSV at any time. We delete it 30 days after you close the account.
