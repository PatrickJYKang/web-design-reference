# Design standard

Build this page so it looks like a skilled designer made it for this particular subject, not like a template with the subject's words dropped in.

Before writing code, decide on one visual direction and commit to it. A page with a point of view that some people dislike is better than one nobody would notice.

- **Type.** Choose typefaces for the subject; Inter, Roboto and the system stack are choices, not defaults. Set a real scale: display sizes with tight leading and tracking, body text at a comfortable measure, and clear steps between levels.
- **Colour.** A small palette with a job for each colour. One accent, used sparingly. Avoid the purple-to-blue gradient on white and the neon-on-near-black look unless the subject calls for it.
- **Layout.** Let the content decide the structure. Avoid the stock sequence of centred hero, three equal feature cards and a gradient call-to-action band. Vary density and rhythm between sections, and use alignment and whitespace before boxes and borders.
- **Detail.** One spacing scale and one radius logic throughout. Prefer hairlines and contrast to drop shadows on everything. No emoji as icons, and no decoration that doesn't carry meaning.
- **Motion.** A few short, purposeful transitions. Nothing that delays reading.
- **Copy.** Use the brief's words. Don't add sections, claims or statistics it doesn't contain.
