## Read
- The frame stays and the content moves: a top bar with a search field about 580px wide and a sidebar about 235px wide hold still while a three-column masonry scrolls for 2873px.
- Black ground, light tiles: every thumbnail sits in a #e7e5e4 frame with 8px corners, about 374px wide at its own height, so column bottoms never line up and the eye runs down, not across.
- Type stays small and hierarchy comes from weight and case: a 24px w700 title in orange, 14px w500 for 65% of the text, 12px w700 uppercase at 0.1em for sidebar group labels, 10px counts right-aligned in each row.
- The top bar holds the only buttons: 34px tall with 6px corners, dark fills with a 1px border, and one gradient fill for the paid tier.

## Weak spots
- A green promotional tile with a video player sits second in the grid at the size of a result and reads as one.
- Thumbnails are too soft at this width to read, and tiles carry no caption.
