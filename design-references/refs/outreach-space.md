## Weak spots
- Text fades in over the scene while it is still mid-blur, and several screens show only a single short passage in 2000px of scroll, so the reader waits on the animation.
- 12px labels at low opacity on a moving background are hard to read, and the page measures 28.7 screens on a phone with the same thin lines.
