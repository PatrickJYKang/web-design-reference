## Read
- One size of type: 15px/1.4 at 0.026em tracking is 99% of the text, so the wordmark top left is the same weight class as every label; nothing is set large.
- The first screen is a single pale grey isometric line drawing of a shelf with small objects, centred on white. It is the only content visible, so the drawing carries the whole message.
- Black is the only ink on white. No borders, radii or shadows; 150ms fades on opacity are the only motion.
- Content spans 1371 of 1440 with 35 to 38px section padding, and the name sits in a fixed 80px nav, so the page hugs the edges rather than centring in a column.

## Weak spots
- The drawing is very pale grey on white and its objects are too small to read at this size.
- The sheet shows no navigation labels or copy, so a visitor cannot tell what the studio does or where to go.
