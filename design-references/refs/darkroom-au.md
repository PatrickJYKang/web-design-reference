## Read
- A wordmark about 1368px wide opens and closes the page in condensed cream capitals; the 40px h1 sits under it and is plain w400, so the logo is the display type.
- Mono is the working face: Suisse Mono at 12px sets 72% of the text, in #b3b2ac on #0d0d0d, with product names and a numbered index of every mockup in the footer. A proportional Suisse takes the headings.
- One saturated colour, #2426fa, appears on the 48px "Shop mockups" button; the secondary 36px buttons are #212121 and every radius is 4px or less.
