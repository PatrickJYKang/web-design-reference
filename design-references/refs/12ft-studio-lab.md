## Read
- A bitmap display face, Pinwheel, is set at 231.3px in #2222ff on a #e5dede ground. It opens the page and returns as a 900px band between pairs of projects, repeating each project's name larger than the page and cropped by the edges.
- Everything else is quiet: Lab Mono at 14px w400 (63% of the text) for labels and descriptions in #171717, with a 28px sans for the intro line.
- Experiments sit in 490px-wide cards, two per row with a 4px gap, each 653px tall at a 48px radius. Grounds alternate between near-black, off-white and the same blue, so each card is one thing in one colour.
- Nav is a 40px fixed corner of two small parts (a round switch and a "Menu" button); two summary cards at the top count case studies and tools, each with a pill-edged button.

## Weak spots
- The cropped 231px titles repeat a name that the card already shows, so the page is mostly spacing.
- The 14px mono labels inside dark cards are small against moving or busy artwork.
