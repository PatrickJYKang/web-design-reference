## Read
- One family, Epilogue, carries everything. The wordmark runs 139px w800 and bleeds off both edges of the first screen, while episode titles sit at 30px w500 and make up 76% of the text.
- The page is a single grid: 21 cards of 398×523 in 3 columns with a 60px gap, 7 rows tall (4045px band). Each card is a square portrait in a #f5f5f5 tile with a two-line title underneath and nothing else, no dates or tags.
- Contrast comes from the ground, not from ornament: white, a #f5f5f5 grid band, then a 1270px black band for a featured studio visit with a 45.6px w800 heading. No borders, no shadows, two pill shapes.
- Navigation is four plain text links top right in a 144px band over a one-line strapline; the only call to action is a small "More interviews" link under the grid.

## Weak spots
- The wordmark is cropped at both edges at the top of the page, so the name is only partly readable in the first view.
- Titles run to three lines at 30px and the portraits are small inside their tiles, which makes the 4045px grid long to scan.
