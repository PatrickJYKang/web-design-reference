## Read
- Body text is 32px/1.75 weight 300, about twice a usual reading size, in a cell about 910px wide: roughly 60 characters a line and eight or nine lines a screen. It is 87% of the text, all #111111.
- The grid is drawn. 1px #111111 lines frame the page 24px in from the edges, cut a strip of eight 174px cells across the top, then a 174px side cell either side of a 1040px text cell, moving from section to section. Each section carries a boxed 12px number at a cell corner.
- The ground marks progress: #ff8126 through the opening, then a gradient through pink to a blue of about #4db5ff by the fourth screen, with the ink unchanged. Pull statements get their own 809px bands, 80px/1.1 centred in a second, typewriter-like face.
- The opening is a cream panel inside the orange frame with the title in condensed serif capitals about 190px tall and a low-poly 3D rock laid over it; byline, date and reading time are 12px uppercase labels. Smaller 3D objects sit on the grid lines further down.

## Weak spots
