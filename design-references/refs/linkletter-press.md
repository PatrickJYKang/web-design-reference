## Read
- Two voices: New Spirit serif for the 60px h1 and 40px section heads (w400, -0.02em), Instrument Sans at 16px for 75% of the text. The serif carries the "printed matter" idea; nothing else is decorative type.
- The page is near-black #1a1a1a on #ffffff and #f6f5f5, with brown #3b2c20 as the only button fill (46px and 48px tall, 12px radius). The one strong colour, a hand-painted orange stroke (about #d9532c, read off the sheet), appears only as artwork around the envelope and the quote card.
- The product is shown as paper: a sideways row of 16 typeset pages, each 278x360 with a 12px radius and 32px gap, bleeds off the screen edge, followed by a flat envelope mockup in the hero.
- Nine bands in 7.7 screens, the first 1579px tall and mostly illustration; grounds alternate white and #f6f5f5 with only 1px #e1e1e1 lines, so rhythm comes from spacing (112 to 176px section padding).

## Weak spots
- The hero band is 1579px tall and the customer logos sit in washed-out grey, so the first two screens give the product little to read.
- The founder's letter near the end runs at 16px across a wide column with no pull quotes or subheads.
