## Read
- The screen is split in half: the left 720px is black with a pinned product photograph on an orange field, and stays put while the right half scrolls. The article is 2.9 screens, so the picture is seen the whole way through.
- The right half is the page's reading column: a centred 56.4px/1.1 title with a smaller grey category under it, then 19.5px/1.4 serif text about 620px wide (86% of the text, read off the sheet). A 13px sans byline sits between title and text.
- A 37px orange (#fa5e00) strip under the nav carries the membership offer, which is the only colour on the right half other than the pictures.
- The end of the page has a full-width purple call-to-action block in the text, then a "Discover more" pair of cards at about 295px wide, each with image and a two-line sans title.

## Weak spots
- The subtitle and date are black at 25% opacity, which is faint on white and hard to read.
- The orange strip and the full-width nav use 14.6px and 13px type that is small against the 56px title.
