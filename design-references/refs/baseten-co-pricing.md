## Read
- There are no tiers with prices. Two 12px-gap cards (about 563px each) share the first screen: a pale-grey "pay as you go" card and a pale-green one for production scale, each with a three-item tick list and a full-width 36px black button.
- Below them the page is a rate sheet, a 4108px band with a 218px sticky left menu (Model APIs, Dedicated inference, Training, Sandboxes) and a 1023px column of tables. Each table opens with a #f1f5f0 header block holding a mono label, a 24px title and a one-line note.
- Rows are 28px of 14px text with 1px #e3eae1 lines, no fills and no radius beyond 4px; prices are $ figures with no emphasis, so the numbers carry the layout.
- Each product line has its own small colour square in the menu (purple, green, blue, orange), and a pink #ffa9fd band near the end is the only loud colour. Type is beausite at weight 350 to 400 with geistMono for labels.

## Weak spots
- Rows have the same weight throughout, so a reader cannot tell a flagship model from a minor one without reading the names.
- The sticky menu items are faint grey except the active one, and are about 10px tall in the sheet.
