## Read
- Type is the whole design: a 135px w400 name in a custom face whose "o" is a pixel ring, centred on cream #fffcf0 in near-black #000310. The same 135px size is also the commonest text (46%), so there is no second size above 14px.
- The page is cut into full-width bands by 1px #000310 rules, each about 150px tall at the 1440 sheet scale. One band holds the name, one a counter ("01" and "04" at 135px either side of a progress bar), one a looping ticker of service words separated by dots.
- Only 1.3 screens of content: a 72px fixed nav of three 10px w700 labels at far left, centre and far right, one 28px outlined square button, and a 12px tagline.
- Motion is slow: 1400ms and 2000ms on cubic-bezier(0.19, 1, 0.22, 1), for the sideways ticker and the expanding bands.

## Weak spots
- Nav labels and the tagline are 10 to 12px against 135px display type; the jump leaves nothing to read at normal size.
