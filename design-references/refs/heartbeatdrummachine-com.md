## Read
- Everything is IBM Plex Mono at w300: 11px copy (93% of text), 16 and 18px uppercase titles, 9px caps for data. With a single weight and nearly a single size, hierarchy comes from position and white space.
- The page alternates a 720px full-bleed image band (a red and white machine in close-up, then each child's photo) with a 630 to 900px band of #f2f2f2 holding only a caps title at the left and two or three narrow text columns, about 255px each. About 19 screens at that rhythm.
- Each patient section repeats: name, a row of data in 9px caps (sequence, Hz, BPM) across the photo's lower edge, then a thin line drawing of concentric circles and a heartbeat trace on the page ground.
- The only controls are two 32px buttons with 8px corners, one #303030 and one #b5262f.

## Weak spots
- The button labels are default link blue #0000ee on the red and the dark fill, which barely reads.
- Light-weight 11px copy in #303030 beside photographs is hard to read at length, and the 9px data row more so.
