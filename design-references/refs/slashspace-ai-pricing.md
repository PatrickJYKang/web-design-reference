## Read
- Nothing is large: h1 28px at weight 400, the price about the same, body 14px, notes 10.5–12.3px, all one sans in warm greys (#292524, #44403b, #57534d) on #f5f5f4. Emphasis comes from space, with section padding up to 258px.
- Left-aligned throughout, and a customer quote with an avatar sits directly under the title, above any price.
- Two ways to pay share one panel (688 + 459px): a yearly price with a dark pill, and a lifetime deal sold elsewhere with a white pill. Orange is spent only on the small label above each and on the discount.
- The feature table has a single value column: label at the left edge, value at mid-width, 1px #e7e5e4 rules, small group heads. The footer repeats the three actions as stacked pills over a faded greyscale landscape.

## Weak spots
- Refund and discount terms are set at 10.5–12.3px in grey on grey, too small for conditions a buyer has to read.
- A black promo strip with orange text above the nav is the highest-contrast thing on a page built to be quiet.
