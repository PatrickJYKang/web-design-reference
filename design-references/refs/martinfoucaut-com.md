## Read
- The whole site is a table. A frame of cells surrounds every screen: a 40px top row of six filters, side rails about 44px wide with rotated labels (local time, mail, registration numbers), and a bottom row of six column heads. Each project's facts sit in cells directly above their heading.
- The grid is drawn by the ground: black cells with a 7.2px radius sit 1px apart on #696969, so the lines are gaps, not borders, and unused area is #242424.
- 23 projects take one 1352×900 screen each, in the same order: a 20px title cell three columns wide, a one-line 13px description, then a strip of six 224px images 1px apart.
- One family and one weight: Arial 400 at 13px for 84% of the text and 20px for titles only. #808080 against #efefef separates a column's label from its value.

## Weak spots
- Under the image strip each screen leaves about 400px of empty #242424 before the facts row, so 23 projects cost 23 full screens.
- 13px Arial drops to 10.1px on the phone, and the 9.7px place names set beside client names are hard to read even at desktop size.
