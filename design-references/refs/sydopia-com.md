## Read
- The first screen is a 36px weight-300 serif statement centred under a small eye mark, with the top of the work row along the bottom edge. The nav is three 16px sans links with no bar behind them.
- Work is one sideways row of 20 cards, 432×325 with 25px gaps and a 4px radius, so about three show at once: a screenshot, then a 24px serif title and a 13px sans link on one line.
- Testimonials are a pile of four tilted paper sheets about 1000px wide with 5px corners, in yellow, pink #ffb1c5, green #36a55a and blue, each with a 32px quote, a 40px italic name and a red oval sticker. They are the only colour fields on the #fffff9 page.
- Sans is kept for labels: the nav, small tracked uppercase heads, and a footer row of four links at 20px, weight 500, 0.2em, spread across the full 1354px between dotted 1px rules.

## Weak spots
- Only about three of the 20 work cards fit the screen, and nothing says how many lie off to the right; on a 2.8-screen page most of the portfolio is out of sight.
- The sheets overlap, so one testimonial is readable at a time and the other three show only as coloured edges.
