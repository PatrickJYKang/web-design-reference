## Read
- Headlines are a serif deck face, uppercase at 50px or 32px centred over a small 150 to 330px image, so the page shows lots of white around small pictures.
- Three grounds only: white, a pale grey-green #dbded5 (11%) and black, with text in black or white; no accent, since the colour comes from cover and fashion photographs.
- A black 54px bar stays fixed at the foot with a login link and a 28px outlined Subscribe button (1px white, 2px radius), so the sale is always on screen without a popup.
- Story rows are 328×540 or 444×571 portrait images in threes 20px apart, with a 13px sans category line and a 15px caption underneath; one row scrolls sideways past the edge.

## Weak spots
- Captions and category lines are 13px and 15px against 50px heads, so the step between them is large and the small text is hard to read on the grey-green ground.
- Full-width photographic hero bands lay a serif title over the picture with low contrast, so the headline and the story list disappear on light areas.
