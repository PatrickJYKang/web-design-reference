## Read
- One project at a time: a single portrait image about 228px wide sits centred in an otherwise empty screen, with only 20% of the first screen covered by image. Whitespace is the layout.
- Everything is brown #644530 on warm #f7f4ef, one ink on one ground, with no borders, radii or shadows. Colour comes from the image alone.
- Type pairs Caslon (15.1px, 72% of text) with Sweet Sans for 10.9px w700 uppercase labels at 0.16em tracking. The project name and its italic discipline list sit bottom left, the labels Info and Projects at the corners.
- A script wordmark centred in a 136px fixed nav, with 720px of content, gives the page a letterhead feel rather than an app feel.

## Weak spots
- The 10.9px tracked labels are very small and the italic credit line is smaller still.
- The page is one screen at 1 to 1 screens, so everything beyond the first project sits behind navigation not shown.
