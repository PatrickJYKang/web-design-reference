## Read
- The ground changes with the story: a 1378px white opening band (a 864px photo and the 40px title), then #94c8ff blue bands for most of the text and quotes, then white again. Text and photographs stay black on both.
- Two voices are paired: the h1 and the 25px pull quote are in a grotesque (Labil, w400, 0.025em), the 17px/1.47 body is in a serif (Bradford, 68% of the text) with letter-spacing, and 13 to 14px sans captions sit in a side column.
- The measure is set in a 630px text column beside a 450px column for captions or images inside the 1080px content area, so image rows (three portraits about 290px wide, in a row) and quotes interrupt the text without moving the column.
- Sections are 550 to 870px tall bands, so the 21 desktop screens are an alternation of text, image row and quote.

## Weak spots
- A consent bar about 300px tall is fixed over the lower third of every screen of the sheet and a dark overlay dims the page above it; the full-width masthead is also dimmed.
- Date, tags and credits at the left are set at about 10 to 13px in pale blue, which is hard to read on white.
