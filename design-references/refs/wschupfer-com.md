## Read
- The work is the page: four 900px bands, each a row of staggered photographs in 360×720 slots (36 in all), spread across the full 1380px and set at different heights so no two sit on the same line.
- Text is almost absent. Untitled Sans at 15px sets 99% of what there is, and the one large element is an 80px logotype at the top left.
- White ground with black as the only ink and no borders, radii or shadows; the images carry all the colour.
- The header is 83px and not pinned: a menu and search icon at the left and a "New York" label at the right, with a small logo fixed top right once scrolled.

## Weak spots
- Images carry no titles or captions in the capture, so a visitor cannot tell what a picture is or who it is for without clicking.
- The rows move sideways, so the page's real length is not clear from a still.
