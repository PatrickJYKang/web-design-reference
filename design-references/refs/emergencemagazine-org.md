## Read
- Pictures come first and words wait: a full-bleed image 1652px tall, nearly two screens, then an 811px photograph carrying one story with its tag, title and byline in white at the bottom left. The largest text on the first screen is 18px.
- No type is larger than 24px. Titles are 24px/1.17 with 0.05em tracking, labels 12px uppercase at 0.117em, and the commonest text is 13px. Tracking and the size of the photographs do the work a big headline would.
- Selling the print volume is a floating panel about 280px wide at the right edge of the hero (title, price, an order link, the cover on black) and not a band of its own.
- After the images the page goes to white space: the 24px statement fills the left half of a 736px band, and the story section is a two-column split (2 × 625, gap 72) with one word at the left (read, listen, watch) and three 199px portrait cards 14px apart at the right, each with a 12px tag above and a 12.3px title below.

## Weak spots
- Card titles are 12.3px with a grey byline run on in the same line, hard to read under images this small.
- In the capture the white 24px title on the pale gradient band at the foot of the page, and on the fade between the two photographs, almost disappears.
