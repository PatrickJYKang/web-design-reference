## Read
- Most of the page is one pinned stage 19,022px tall, so scrolling plays an animation instead of moving down a document: rings of the product's name spin in the hero's dark right half and widen to fill the screen, then a 265px counter climbs from "~00" while a mono ledger of lost hours fills in line by line beside it.
- Mono is the reading face: GeistMono is 72% of the text at 13.7px/1.62, uppercase for labels and nav. The sans appears only in headings at 45.9px/1.1 w400 and in the counter.
- Two inks, #232323 and paper #f1eee7, swap as ground and text; the current ledger row is marked by inverting it to a dark bar, and the tan #d6a878 is 6% of the text.
- The nav is a dark rounded box about 510px wide floating at top centre: six mono links over a one-line scrolling ticker.

## Weak spots
- A single argument is stretched over about 21 screens of pinned scrolling, and the email sign-up box surfaces half-hidden under the character-dither transition between the pale and dark sections.
- 13.7px mono for two-thirds of the text (12px on phone) is tiring at length.
