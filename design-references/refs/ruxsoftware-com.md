## Read
- One typeface, VC Nudge, with w700 at 80/1.1, 60/1 and 30/1.1 and tight tracking (-0.01em); w400 only for the 21px and 16px copy. The headings are heavy and stacked in tight leading while the body stays light.
- Two inks: cream #ece9e4 and dark brown #291a02 alternate as grounds, with text swapping between them, in bands of 690 to 860px. Black appears only under the hero.
- The seam between cream and brown is a stippled dot gradient, a dithered edge about 60px tall, used at three or more joins, so bands blend instead of cutting.
- The hero is orange line art of a crane running behind and past the 80px headline, and the later bands pair 563px text with 793px photographs of real equipment and crews (tiles 678x347).
- A 105px sticky nav on cream with five links and a brown "Contact" button; a 206px brown announcement bar sits above the headline.
