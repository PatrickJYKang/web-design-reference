## Read
- The list is the page. Each participant is a ruled row: a 12px caption cell, a thumbnail cell, then a 120px serif name and title that runs past the right or left edge of the screen. Rows sit in a 2160px band and drift sideways against each other.
- Structure is 1px black rules only, on #fafafa with #000000 text; no fills, no shadows, 48px side and top padding.
- The sans is small and plain: 16px for 71% of text, 12px captions, 10.7px bold caps in the fixed footer line. The only large size is the 120px serif.
- Past the list, the names rotate and scatter among the thumbnails in a free collage, a 900px band of rotated words and images.
- The fixed 48px bar holds one centred "watch the seminar" link, a language switch and a round info button.

## Weak spots
- Titles are cropped at the screen edge, so a visitor reads names but rarely the full work title.
- Captions are 12px beside 120px names, a 10-to-1 jump that makes the real information hard to find.
