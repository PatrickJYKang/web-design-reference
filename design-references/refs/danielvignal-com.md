## Read
- The identity is not at the top. Name, role, three links and four filters form one block about 335px wide, fixed at the vertical middle of the screen to the right of the pictures, and the stills scroll past it.
- Stills run in two 424px columns 20px apart, set about 200px in from the left edge. Heights follow each frame's ratio, from about 220px to about 530px, so the columns drift out of step.
- All type is one family at two sizes: 18px for name and role, 13px for links and filters, weight 500 with −0.03 to −0.042em tracking. Inactive filters are the same ink at 50%, and the current link has a 1px underline.
- Tiles have no radius, border or shadow; a #222222 fill at 6% holds each frame's place until it loads.

## Weak spots
- On desktop a still shows no title or credit. The phone layout sets a 20px title and a grey director's name under each one, and says more for it.
- The filter row is 13px at 50% ink, the only control beside six screens of pictures, and easy to miss.
