## Read
- The page is laid out like an app, not a shop: a 145px category sidebar (Mockups, Bundles, Graphics, Tutorials, with counts), a 190px-wide information card and a 350px-wide media column, all in dark panels on #0d0d0d with 4px corners.
- The buy control is the only saturated UI colour: a full-width blue "Add to Cart" button above three licence tabs (Standard, Commercial, Corporate), with Specs, Features and Licensing folded in rows below.
- Type is small and quiet: a 24px w400 title, then 12.8px for most text and 10px uppercase labels, in a Suisse sans with a mono for counts. All colour comes from the full-bleed renders, which alternate grey and saturated green backgrounds.
- Images run in a single column 340px wide, each at roughly 350x235, followed by a "Related" row of two 342px tiles with mono-style captions.

## Weak spots
- Sidebar and info card use about 12px grey (#b3b2ac) text on near-black, and licence tabs and labels are small enough to be hard to read.
- The wordmark fixed at the bottom of the sidebar uses a lot of space for no function.
