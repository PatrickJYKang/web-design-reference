## Read
- The whole site is a list: 57 project titles in four columns (web, graphic design, writing, type design), each 239px wide and 16px apart, every row a 14px link over a 1px black rule at a pitch of about 29px. No project gets a picture, date or description here.
- One size, one weight, one colour: 14px/1.43 system sans in #000000 on #ffffff. The name and the four column heads are told apart only by a soft blur on the letters.
- The images are a frame, not content: project thumbnails, tilted and overlapping, hang over all four edges of the viewport and stay there while the list scrolls. They make up the 31% image share without entering the 1312px content area.
- A small orange block-shaped mascot sits fixed at the bottom right, the only object inside the frame.

## Weak spots
- The blurred column heads read as a rendering fault before they read as headings.
- The thumbnails are cropped to slivers by the viewport edge, so none of the work can be judged without following a link.
