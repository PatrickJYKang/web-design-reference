## Read
- The issue front is one 1440×900 screen with no scroll: a full-viewport canvas of a block city in orange-red haze, with every piece of text laid over it in #ffffff.
- All the text laid on the scene is 12.8px. Source Serif 4 at 1.05 leading sets two short paragraphs on a 340px measure; IBM Plex Mono capitals set the issue title, the contents list and three outlined pill links 17px tall. The scene supplies the scale and the type stays at caption size.
- Text hangs from an 8-column grid at the quarter lines (x 370, 730, 1090) with 10px gutters. The first quarter holds a black rounded card, about 345×190px, with the journal name, issue title, DOI, date and a white "More info" button.
- The contents list is two mono columns, author then title. Only the first row is sharp; the rows under it are blurred, and the measured 1000ms transitions on filter suggest they clear one at a time.

## Weak spots
- 12.8px serif at 1.05 leading over a picture is hard to read; the lines of the two paragraphs nearly touch.
- With the list blurred, a visitor cannot see what the issue contains without stepping through it.
