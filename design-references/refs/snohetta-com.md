## Read
- One family at weight 300 for every size, from the 60px statement to the 16px footer, on #ffffff with no fills. Rank is carried by three text colours (#000000 for names, #757575 and #949494 for descriptors, labels and dates), not by weight.
- The work is listed in words as much as shown: the disciplines and about nine highlighted projects are 48px/1.3 text rows under 1px #dfdfdf rules, each project name in black followed on the same line by its descriptor in grey.
- Two left edges organise the page: section labels in small grey start at x 40 and content starts at about x 507 ([310 + 893, gap 157]). The wordmark and "Menu" float on those same two edges in place of a nav bar (0px).
- Images come in unequal sizes, not a grid: five photographs of different heights in the opening strip, news cards 427 and 660 wide. The events list is five 1360×74 ruled rows of event, location and date.

## Weak spots
- Project descriptors run off the right edge mid-word, so most of the grey half of each 48px row cannot be read.
- A grey notice card about data settings sits bottom-left over the content on four of the six screens.
