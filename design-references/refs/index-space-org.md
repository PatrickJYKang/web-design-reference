## Read
- One device styles everything: a 1px #000000 outline with square corners. It draws the buttons, the 460 and 940-wide location cards, the resource thumbnails and the rules between events. Grounds stay #ffffff with no shadows, and the only fill is one #ffffa5 callout band.
- Buttons are label-sized on purpose: 27px tall with 5px side padding and 16px text, set in joined pairs ("More Information" / "Join") and the same size in the nav as in a card, so no action outranks another.
- One family at weight 400 over five sizes (32, 24, 20, 16, 13) with 20px/1.3 as the reading size (67%). Headings are only 4 to 12px above body, and secondary lines drop to 60% black.
- A centred column of 940 or 1180, with three bands that scroll sideways on x-snap (20 slides of 1180×598, 15 quote cards at 460, 20 slides at 940), keeps a long programme to 7.5 screens.
