## Read
- Two voices with a clean split: a serif at weight 500 for the 56px title, plan names and prices (numerals about 45px with a small raised currency sign), Inter at 15–16px for everything that explains.
- The plans float on colour. A peach-to-lilac gradient fills a rounded panel behind the four cards (286×626, 32px apart, radius 28px, white at 90%) and returns behind the closing call; between them the page is plain white.
- The one-off licence is kept out of the grid: a full-width card under the subscriptions with its own price and button, so buying once does not become a fifth column.
- Buttons are 47px pills, black fill for the self-serve plans and a 1px black outline for the two sales-led ones. In the table the plan header pins with name, price and button; marks are small circled ticks and dashes with no colour.

## Weak spots
- A "switching tools" prompt stays fixed at the bottom right of every screen and covers part of the fourth plan card and the table.
- An 80px dark promo strip sits above the nav, so the page opens on an announcement for something else.
