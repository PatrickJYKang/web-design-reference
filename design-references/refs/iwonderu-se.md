## Read
- Each 900px band shows one picture, pushed to the left or right edge of the screen, with the rest left as bare #f1f1f1; the photo's position alternates band to band, so the eye zigzags down the page.
- The caption is two lines at the picture's inner edge, a client in 10px uppercase and the photographer in 18px, over a pair of 1px rules. Two lines tell a viewer who commissioned the work and who shot it.
- A top 200px fixed block holds a large dark blackletter monogram at left and a 10px uppercase strip below it (studio name, "Est.", a service line, then work, about and contact), so the identity is the only graphic besides photos.
- Neutral greys (#f1f1f1, #fbfafa, #464646 text) keep the page quiet; only later bands change ground to #16140e, #990000 and #e6b8af to match the photo shown.

## Weak spots
- The caption lines are small (10px uppercase) and light grey against the #f1f1f1 ground, so credits are easy to miss.
- A 200px block that stays fixed over the photos takes a large share of the phone's height.
