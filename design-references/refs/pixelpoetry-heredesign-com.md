## Read
- The tool fits one screen with no scrolling: a title block, a 405px three-step rail on the left, an 863px text panel on the right and a black footer bar.
- Two inks, #000000 on #f3edea, with no fills, shadows or transitions. Every control is a square 2px black outline 44px tall with its label centred.
- One pixel face at 16px carries 93% of the text. The 48px uppercase title is the only large thing, and step names are about 20px.

The text panel has a window-style title bar with three dots, one filled, counting the same three stages. On a phone the rail becomes a bar pinned to the bottom showing only the current step.
