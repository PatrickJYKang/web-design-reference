## Read
- Everything is w400 in one grotesk (Die Grotesk), ranked by size alone: 92.2px for statements, 60 to 80px for section lines, 20px for body, with no bold anywhere. Statements are written as sentences and cropped by the viewport as they scroll.
- Colour lives in graphics, not in type or buttons. Tall bars of flat green, cyan, red, yellow, maroon, blue and orange are stacked into abstract block figures that fill the 900px first screen and recur as dividers; text stays #000000 and the page is white with an #ececec band.
- Photos are large and uncaptioned: 7 bands of 1200 to 1630px each hold a single image or a pair at 70 to 94% of the band, placed with a headline beneath it.
- The 64px nav is a white fixed bar with the studio name at left and a search icon and menu at right; the only other chrome is pill-shaped links.

## Weak spots
- Light grey #bebebe link text ("Meet the team", "Our approach") on white is faint.
- With bands of 1500 to 1600px and few anchors, the 14.9 screens are long to navigate for so little text.
