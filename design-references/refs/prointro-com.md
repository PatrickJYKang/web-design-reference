## Read
- Each band shows the product instead of describing it: a drawn phone with the same sample card appears in the hero (534px of text beside a 626px mock-up), three problem cards, a three-phone spread, three steps and a feature grid.
- One sans does everything. Heads are 56px and 48px at weight 800 with −0.03em tracking on 1.04 leading; body is 16–19px at 400. A mint brush stroke under two or three words of a heading is the only ornament.
- Colour is cream plus one green: grounds #fbfaf7, #fffcf7 and #f9f5ef, cards #f9f5ef with 16px corners, and #22524c kept for the primary button (50px tall, 10px corners) and the mock-up's own buttons.
- Bands are divided by 1px #e7e0d5 rules, and the hero's two halves by a vertical one, so the page reads as ruled cells, not floating sections.

## Weak spots
- The same sample person and card repeat in more than a dozen mock-ups across 11.6 screens; by the feature grid the images stop adding information.
- The three cream grounds differ too little to mark a new section without the rules.
