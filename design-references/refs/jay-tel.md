## Read
- One size does all the work: 17px/1.41 weight 400 for the name, section labels, rows and links. Rank comes from brightness alone: row names in #ff9900, their one-line descriptions in #bd730d, lower-case section labels dimmer again.
- The palette is one orange hue at different strengths: brown #473024 ground, amber text, and a commit grid of 2px-radius squares shaded in the same amber by count. The only other colour is a round album cover, about 48px, riding the rule under "listening to".
- A 656px column centred on 1440 with nothing above about 460px, so the name row sits at mid-screen. Each section is a two-column list: names in a column about 144px wide, descriptions beside, rows about 40px apart.
- Live data stands in for pictures: a local-time clock opposite the name, the track now playing, and the grid of recent commits.

## Weak spots
- Most of the text is #bd730d on #473024, about 3.3:1, and the section labels are fainter still.
- The top half of the first screen is empty ground; a visitor sees a name and three rows.
