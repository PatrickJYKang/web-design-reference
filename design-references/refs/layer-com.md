## Read
- One size does the talking: a 34px serif w400 is 58% of the text, used for headings and paragraphs alike. Only the 14px uppercase mono labels (nav, "you're viewing") differ.
- Each band is a full-screen scene of 860 to 1125px, black ground with a photo or video of a room holding one artwork. Copy sits in a column about 290px wide at the lower left, inside a 867px content width.
- Chrome is nearly absent: a fixed 128px nav with no ground, mono links at 14px, and one square white button, 48px tall, radius 0, "Reserve yours $99" at top right. It is the only filled element on the page.
- Colour comes only from the artworks. UI is white and #b4b4b4 on black, with 1px white borders at 30% opacity.

## Weak spots
- Paragraphs of 34px serif over moving photographs lose contrast where the image is bright, as on the blue scene.
- Nav labels are dim 14px mono on dark and hard to pick out against the photos.
