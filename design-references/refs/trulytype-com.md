## Read
- The interface is Helvetica at small sizes (12px w500 is the commonest text, h1 only 32px), so the fonts being sold are the only large type on the page. Each card shows a specimen at about 40 to 60px in that font and a 12px line for the weights available.
- Products sit in a 2-column grid: 12 cards of 672×500 with a 16px gap, #f2f2f2 fill, 12px radius, no text border. Each card ends in two pill buttons (outline "Learn more", filled "License now") whose fill is a different colour per font: orange, green, blue, pink, red, purple.
- The specimen is faded to grey in the sheet, so colour belongs to the buttons and not to the cards; this gives each product an identity colour without changing the layout.
- Glossy 3D frog renders in green, blue and red overlap cards and the hero at random, the only photographic or rendered imagery on the page. The hero is one grey 900px panel with a weight list, and the footer is a black band of link columns.

## Weak spots
- Specimen text and the "Discover our collection" label are grey (#999999) on #f2f2f2, and the buttons are washed out in the sheet, so contrast is low.
- A cookie notice is pinned to the lower right of every screen over the cards.
