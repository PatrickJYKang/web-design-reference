- Photography on black is the whole design. The first screen is one photograph, the device at a raking angle; lifestyle photographs follow edge to edge, two across, 7px apart. Images cover 96% of the page above the footer.
- Type behaves like the legend printed on a device: short stacked lines in caps, each underlined by a hairline, placed on the photograph, with Japanese sub-labels beside them.
