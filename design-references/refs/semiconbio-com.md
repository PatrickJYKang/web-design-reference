## Read
- Headlines are the design: 90 to 100px at weight 400 and −0.01em, three lines each, with 16px copy in two small columns beneath, so the size jump is about 6 to 1.
- Black and #e5e5e5 bands alternate (a 1615px grey band between black ones), and the page is almost all white or grey text on near-black #020202 with no accent colour.
- Numbered section labels in small mono (N01, N02) sit on a rule above each headline, in a 1300px column, giving a technical-paper structure.
- Scroll is staged: a 1615px pinned band holds three 350×490 cards with 1px #ffffff@20% outlines and a coloured dot each, and a 12000px pinned stage follows; the page runs about 24 screens on both desktop and phone.

## Weak spots
- A 12000px pinned stage means long scrolling for little content; on the sheet its screens are nearly empty black with tiny labels.
- Small uppercase labels at about 8 to 10px grey on black are close to unreadable.
