## Read
- The hero is a pinned 900px photograph with no bar. A four-line statement at 28.7px runs edge to edge across the top and ends in the four nav words, underlined; a serif wordmark with an x-height of about 150px sits on the bottom edge.
- After the hero the nav is plain 34px text at top right, four words separated by commas, opposite a wordmark of the same height. No ground, no buttons, no icons.
- Each project gets about 950px of white and one or two photographs placed differently each time: a 716px image between 354px margins, or two 712px columns 16px apart with the images hung at different heights. Photographs cover 49 to 74% of a band.
- The caption is three pairs on one line at mid-height: name at the left edge, location near the centre, type at the right, each a 10px label raised beside a 14.7px value.

## Weak spots
- The location label sits mid-row, so it usually lands on the photograph, where small dark text over wood or shadow cannot be read.
- The hero's white statement crosses pale curtains and fades into them for part of every line.
