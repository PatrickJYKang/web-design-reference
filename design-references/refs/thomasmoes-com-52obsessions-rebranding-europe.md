## Read
- The title is 150px/1 at w800 in white on #3624e7 blue, with a 28px dek under it and a field of yellow star shapes filling the rest of the first screen. Nothing else on the page is that large; chapter heads drop to 66px.
- The ground changes with the argument: blue for the intro, #1f1a13 and black for "the shift", white for the "nested identities" diagram. A sticky sub-nav of seven chapter names stays under the 40px top bar, so the colour change is also a place marker.
- Text and picture sit side by side: a 560px text card (radius 20px) beside a pinned 450 to 687px illustration (a yellow star, a white cross, an arrow in a ring) that changes while the text scrolls. Key phrases are set at 66px in yellow or blue within black.
- The article runs 16.8 screens on desktop and 21.2 on the phone, with a sideways row of 19 cards at 340×530 and a second row of four 600px panels.

## Weak spots
- Body copy in the narrow cards is about 18px in a 280px column, about 30 characters a line, beside a large empty illustration area.
- Sections of 300px padding and 1,000px+ pinned stages make the argument hard to skim; the reader cannot see more than one idea at a time.
