## Read
- The whole page is one size: 56px w500 Helvetica Neue for 97% of the text, with headlines run together as inline text, each one wrapped in a 2px black outline with 3px corners.
- A yellow-to-orange gradient cover (#ffc33b to #ffba42) opens the page with a single small line and a Start arrow, then everything after it is white with black text.
- A 12-item month list (January to December) is pinned at the left of the content, and the current month turns orange, which is the only navigation.

## Weak spots
- Line-height 1.8 with outlines on each headline makes the lines overlap their own boxes, so rules clip descenders and run into the line below.
- The phone drops everything to 20.8px, so the one-size scale loses its drama on small screens.
