## Read
- Photography carries the page: each of 11 bands is about 900px tall with a photo at 52 to 100% of the area, and the text is one 22px uppercase line (0.05em tracking) in the corner or over the picture.
- Type is two wide faces in one size system: 48px uppercase for the masthead and hero title, 22px uppercase for 49% of the text, 12px uppercase for buttons and small labels, and 16px for the little left over; all black or white, no colour.
- Navigation is a 79px fixed white bar with the masthead centred and three links (Search, Archive, About) at the right, split from the photos by a 1px black rule.
- Bands alternate between full-bleed photos, a 720px-wide pair of images side by side, and a single inset image with a caption link beside it (a small label then a name and arrow).
