## Read
- The index is one screen that scrolls sideways. An intro block about 300px wide sits at the left (a glowing blue disc with the number, a title in two faces, three centred lines, a "read more" link), then 19 cards run right from x 500, newest first, numbered down from 19.
- Each card is its own cover: 340px wide, 20px radius, with its own ground (#0c7d55 green, a saturated blue, black), its own text colour and its own display face (a light grotesque, a bold one, an extra-wide heavy one in yellow). The layout inside is fixed: number at 80px, title at 30px, a 20px/1.4 summary, a date.
- The page stays out of the way so the cards can differ: #0d0c16 ground, a round mark top left, one 44px white pill top right, no nav and nothing below the row.
- Cards are 30px apart and the third is cut by the viewport edge, which is the only sign that the row continues.

## Weak spots
- Nineteen entries in one sideways row means the oldest are many swipes away, with no list or jump.
- On the phone the row sits below the intro at 280px a card and the page still runs only 1.3 screens, so one card shows at a time.
