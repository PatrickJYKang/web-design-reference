## Read
- There are no plan cards: each plan's name, one-line description, 30px price and 36px pill button form the header cells of one table, columns [270 + 4 × 208] across an 1100 column, and the feature rows carry straight on beneath them for 1818px.
- The table is drawn in 1px #f2f2f2 and #e8e8e8 lines, and the same lines frame the 1100 column down the whole page, nav and footer included, so the page reads as one ruled sheet on #ffffff.
- Type stays small and level: a left-aligned 38px w500 h1, 30px section heads, then 15px/1.6 at #1a1a1a@65% for 91% of the text. Feature groups (Usage, Agent, Collaboration) are rows with a label and empty cells, not headings.
- Blue appears twice, both at the top: the fill of the first plan's button and the "Save 20%" note in the billing toggle. The other three buttons are white pills with an arrow, and the only image is a grainy navy and blue band, 1100 wide, before the footer.

## Weak spots
- The plan header does not stay in view, which leaves two screens of ticks with no column names above them.
- Most rows carry the same tick in all four columns, adding length without helping a choice; on the phone the page grows from 4.9 to 14.2 screens.
