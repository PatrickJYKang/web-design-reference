## Read
- The index is nothing but tiles: 18 squared-off 480×634 covers in 3 columns and 6 rows, flush with no gaps, radii or borders, so the grid is the whole layout.
- Each tile is either a flat saturated field (orange, lime, #00ff59, #fff677, #6475a8, grey) or a photograph; text tiles carry a 24px centred title at the top and a tiny author line at the bottom, which makes each one read as a book cover.
- The masthead is the only large type: 75.6px uppercase, one line, across the full width above the grid; everything else is 24px or 13.3px in black, in one sans at weight 400.
- Colour is the only variety: with no accent and no other ink, the tile fills do all the work of telling items apart.

## Weak spots
- Author names are about 10px on the cover tiles, too small to read against the saturated fills.
- Titles on photo tiles are absent, so those items can't be told apart without opening them.
