## Read
- The whole page is one screen with no nav, scroll or footer: a glowing, barrel-distorted CRT picture 1059px wide, centred on #000000 and covering 71% of the viewport.
- One size and one ink. All of the terminal text is 16px in #ff8c33; emphasis is by inversion (an orange bar with dark letters for the banner, the selected menu row and the buttons), never by size or weight.
- Inside the screen it is a two-column terminal: a caption and an ASCII-art diagram on the left two thirds, a numbered five-item menu with key hints in square brackets on the right, and a prompt line with a "SEND" button along the bottom.
- On a phone the same picture is redrawn, not shrunk: one column, the menu folded into a two-column grid under the diagram, still a single screen.

## Weak spots
- Glow, scanlines and curvature blur the smaller rows; the lower-case lines beside the prompt are hard to read at 16px, and harder at 13px on a phone.
- Everything past the five menu items is behind a key press, so a visitor who does not use the keyboard sees one screen and a link out.
