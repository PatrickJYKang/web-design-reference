## Read
- The name is the only loud element: 64px at w900 as measured, though the sheet shows it spanning the full width in two words (KEVIN, HIPKE) above a large photograph, so it is probably scaled to fit. The h1 itself is the quiet 40px w400 line, so weight, not size, picks the hero.
- Nav is a floating 47px pill group with 12px blur and a 35% tint, kept in view over the work. Buttons and tags are pills with 4% fills or 1px 14% borders, so they recede into the paper grounds.
- Grounds are three near-whites (#f9f9f9, #f4f3ee, #efeee8) and text is #11110f at three opacities; colour comes only from the work thumbnails (navy, purple, olive), so the work is the palette.
- Work is a three-column grid of cards of unequal height (530 and 484 high rows seen), numbered 01 to 12, each with a pill client name and a pill discipline label. A second numbered row sells templates with prices.
