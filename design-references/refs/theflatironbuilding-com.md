## Read
- The page is one screen: a photograph of the building fills 100% of it and arrows at the middle of both edges change the picture. The 1440 by 900 screen holds no body copy.
- All type is brown #805a4b or black, in two tiny caps sizes: 14px at 0.26em and 10px at 0.3em tracking. The name sits centred at the top in two lines.
- The only control is "Inquire", a 29px-tall square button with a 1px #805a4b outline, top right. Press and the fair-housing notice sit in the bottom corners as 10px caps, so legal text is placed on the photo, not in a footer.
- The 116px nav has no ground, so the text sits directly on the sky; the sheet shows a left-hand logo that is almost invisible against it.

## Weak spots
- Brown 10px caps over a changing photograph will fall below readable contrast on some slides.
- Every route is reached through a single "Inquire" button, with nothing on the page to say what is offered.
