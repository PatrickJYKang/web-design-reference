## Read
- One family, Helvetica Neue at w400 throughout, with 16px as the commonest size. The only large text is a 51.8px centred statement in a 900px band, three lines on an off-white #faf9f6 ground.
- Work is shown as 1100px full-bleed photo bands with a 50px service name set over each (logo design, web design, digital marketing); the type sits on the photo, not beside it.
- The first screen is all image (100%, largest text 14px): a laptop photo with a thumbnail strip along the bottom, so the opening is navigation and photography with almost no copy.
- Navigation is a small dark pill docked at the bottom of the screen with a menu icon and a service label, alongside tiny 14px top-corner links (clock, language, "start a project").
- A client-logo row (48 × 200px cells, gap 100) sits between statement and work, in mixed marks that are left uncoloured.

## Weak spots
- Text laid over photographs with a dark scrim is hard to read where the photo is busy, such as the 50px service names.
- The tiny 14px corner links and a small dock label are weak targets and low contrast.
