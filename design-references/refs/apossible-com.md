## Read
- One fixed 900px screen split about 860/580. The left is a #1f1f1f canvas with a faint grid, scattered cards joined by thin curved lines round one large circle, and zoom controls; the right is a #f1f1ee panel that holds all the reading and scrolls by itself.
- The archive is the opening image: references, interviews and experiments appear as cards of different sizes (toned-down photographs and tan note cards, radius 3 to 4px), each with a 12px mono type label at its foot.
- Two voices with fixed jobs: Neue Haas Unica for sentences (25px/1.2 intro, 16px body) and IBM Plex Mono at 12px for data (65% of text: uppercase section labels, dates, categories, card labels).
- The panel is built like a document: the wordmark in a 40px white dropdown box, label-over-paragraph blocks about 480px wide, a one-line email field with a 40px square button, then a three-column table of title, date and category.

## Weak spots
- Card photographs are small, dimmed and cut by the canvas edges, and their only caption is 12px mono, so little can be identified before clicking.
- On desktop the page never scrolls as a whole (1 screen), so the list of outputs starts at the very bottom of the panel, below the intro and the newsletter form.
