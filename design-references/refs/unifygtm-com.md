## Read
- Headings are a tight serif (Feature Text, 21% of the characters): a one-line, left-aligned 90px h1 at weight 700 and −0.04em, 72px statements in the same cut, then weight 400 for 40px sub-statements and stat lines. Body and controls are Saans at 14 to 16px.
- One accent, #fe3c01, used as ink on chosen words inside the serif heads, on the rating stars and on thin vertical rules beside stats. It is never a button fill: buttons are near-square #241e20 rectangles with about 3px corners.
- Every photograph is motion-blurred in dark brown and orange. The hero is a 1240px photo directly under the h1 with a white prompt box floating in its middle, so the product's input stands in for a call to action (59% image); the same blur returns as full-bleed dark strips behind white panels.
- The ground is a warm off-white, #fffff9, for 94% of the page, with few cards. Customer results are a five-column mosaic of 235px tiles 16px apart: portrait photos with a serif stat over them, mixed with #f8f7f3 tiles carrying a logo at the top and a stat at the bottom.

## Weak spots
- In the mosaic the white serif stats sit across the faces in the photo tiles and are hard to read there.
- The hero has no sub-line, and its only action is the prompt box, whose placeholder is small pale grey text.
