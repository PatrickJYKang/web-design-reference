## Read
- The page is a sequence of full-screen sectors: eight 901px bands (beauty, innovation, design, fashion, taste and others), each a full-bleed image with one centred serif word of about 60px, so the whole page is a list of disciplines and no case study is shown in the first nine screens.
- The first screen is white, with the studio name stacked in two lines of about 80px light serif capitals and a tiny sans line ("a global creative office") in 13px, so it reads as a masthead.
- Under each word, a 13px "View ..." button and a strip of six thumbnails (about 30px square) hint at the work inside; nothing else competes with the picture.
- Two voices: Futura PT Book (82% of the text) for the interface and Items MT Light for display, with the 85px fixed nav reduced to a tiny logo at the left and a menu icon at the right.

## Weak spots
- White-on-photo words over bright flowers and oranges lose contrast.
- The thumbnail strips and the "View" buttons are about 13px and about 30px high, small and low-contrast to use.
