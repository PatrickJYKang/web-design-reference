## Read
- One text size does everything: 14px, w400, 0.175em tracking, 100% of the text, in black on white. Hierarchy comes from position and the 480px photo, not from type.
- Ten identical 900px bands, each a full screen split into three 480px columns: one photo, a project name, and a place and year. The photo switches sides from band to band.
- Captions sit in the middle of the screen, 14px uppercase for the name and lower-case for place and date, with large empty white around them. The header ("Oliver du Puy") and footer ("Architecture and Interiors") are two small lines pinned to the top and bottom edges.
