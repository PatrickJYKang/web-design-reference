## Read
- A 139.5px h1 at 0.78 leading and -0.03em fills the screen's centre column in four lines. A 94px and a 53px step follow, then 17.8px w500 for nearly all copy, with almost nothing between 24px and 53px.
- A narrow left rail carries the small pitch and button while the headline sits in a column starting about 250px in; the 842px column against the 1388px frame leaves the left third for captions, quotes and "Success Stories" labels.
- The page alternates warm grey #e8e8e3 and near-black #080807 bands. The black ones carry a 1800px pinned stage where an all-caps line scrolls sideways past a small photo.
- Case studies are 667px-wide photo cards with a title, a one-line brief and one figure ("21%") at the right, repeated six times at 477px per band. Client logos run in a 4-column grid with 8px mono captions.

## Weak spots
- Mono captions and logo labels are about 8 to 10px and nearly unreadable in the sheet.
- The nav sits over the photos with no ground, so its links fade into the hero image on the second screen.
