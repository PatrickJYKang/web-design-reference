## Read
- Reading text gets the generosity, headings do not: body is 16px/1.75 in brown #55473d on #fbf9f5 in a column about 720px wide, under a 29.6px w600 h1 and 22.4px h2s at weight 400.
- Rust #b13616 is the only accent and always means "go here": bold underlined links in the body, the current page as a filled bar in the sidebar, the current section in the right-hand rail. It is 17% of the text because the page is mostly a list of links, each followed by a few words on what is behind it.
- The sidebar shows the whole manual at once: six open groups, 19 pages, 14.4px w600 group names over 13px links, all inside one screen with no scrolling.
- One screenshot of the product, about 720px wide, sits in the first screen; the only other object is a callout on #f1ece3 with a rust left edge.

## Weak spots
- Body links are bold, underlined and coloured at once, and over a dozen of them start consecutive lines, so the list is louder than the headings around it.
