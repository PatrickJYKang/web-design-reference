## Read
- The page is narrow on purpose: everything, the nav included, sits in a 976px column and the hero copy in 624px, leaving about 230px of bare paper each side at 1440.
- The palette is warm paper throughout: three near-identical grounds (#fbf9f5, #fdfcf9, #f1ece3) and two browns for text (#201712, #55473d) where most pages use black and grey. Only the black offer strip at the top and the #102b17 footer leave it.
- Small hand-drawn flowers, shells and leaves in flat pink, blue, yellow, red and green hang off the edges of the column, about one a screen. They are the only illustration and the only saturated colour outside the product.
- The features are #f1ece3 tiles with about 24px corners, two across: a 19px title and three lines of small text top left, then a working fragment of the app on a white card below. The founder's note before them switches to a left-aligned 48px head over two 512px columns, so it reads as a letter, not a feature.
