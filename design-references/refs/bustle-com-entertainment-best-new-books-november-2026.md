## Read
- The title block is split across the width: a 48px w400 Acta h1 and a tiny dek and byline in the left 280px, with a 576px-wide collage hero at the right of the first screen (a 864px band overall).
- The reading column is about 850px of the 1320 content width, set at 18.4px/1.75 in a serif. Each book is a 32px entry head (italic title, author in roman) over a 1px rule, with a blurb, then a cover (about 270px wide) beside a full-width red #db3336 "See on Bookshop" button 51px tall.
- The same four-part unit repeats 11 times (head, blurb, cover, buy button), so the 21.3 phone screens are mostly cover and button, and the red is the only colour besides covers.
- Ads and a related-stories grid (4 × 159x236 cards) are inserted among the entries, and a fixed bottom banner strip repeats on every screen.

## Weak spots
- A full-width animated banner pinned to the bottom edge, a 300x600 ad and a video ad between entries break the reading flow.
- Covers sit on the left with the buy button far to their right and a gap between, so the button is not clearly tied to the cover.
