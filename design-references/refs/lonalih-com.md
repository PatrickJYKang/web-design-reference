## Read
- The page is one screen on both desktop and phone: a scanned, stained cream ground (#fcf4e3 and #faeddc, about 25% of the screen each) with a centred stack of three blue ellipses and a painting between them.
- Every line of text is the same: American Typewriter Condensed at 21.6px w900 in pale lavender #d9d9ff on #00009c, so a single size and a single colour pair carries the whole page.
- Text sits in hand-cut ellipses and a rounded blue shape, 689px to 789px wide, with irregular edges. Each ellipse is one instruction (for example, click the painting to open a release), so the shapes act as buttons and captions at once.
- A 4-radius painting about 200px wide is the only picture and the only link into the work, standing in for navigation and a nav bar.

## Weak spots
- Lavender text at 21.6px on saturated blue is readable, but the thin typewriter letterforms blur at small size.
- Calls to action are written in the ellipses, so nothing distinguishes a link from a caption.
