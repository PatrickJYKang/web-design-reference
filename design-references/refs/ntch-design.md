## Read
- The site is one screen on desktop and phone: a black field (#111111 54%, #000000 15%) with a greyscale cartoon scene in the middle, about 360px wide at 1440, framed by a soft vignette and rgb-split edge slivers like a worn screen.
- The page's own text is 16px in white (100% of the measured text); the bold yellow lettering ("Hello my name is", the name, the role) is part of the artwork, not set text.
- Colour comes only from small stickers: a yellow lemon logo at top left and a green "open to work" diamond at bottom right, so the whole screen has one yellow and one green against grey.
- No scroll, nav or transitions are measured; the introduction and the call to action are carried by the illustration and the two corner badges.

## Weak spots
- The scene's text is part of an image, so it cannot be read by assistive tech or resized.
- The picture is about 25% of the screen, with the rest black; the name and role are small on a large display.
