## Read
- A 72px w500 serif title (Martina Plantijn) opens a long read; the body is an 18px serif at line-height 1.56 in a 1040px column, justified, which is why 73% of the text is one size.
- A small sans nav (Suisse) sits as a fixed left sidebar: name, an In full or In brief toggle, then About, CV, Read, Contact and the case studies, with the current item in orange.
- Ground is #f7f7f7 (76%) with #202020 text and #595959 for captions, so contrast stays soft and the one orange link colour is the only accent.
- Figures break the text at full 1040px width and 650px tall: phone mockups on a pale blue gradient, a dark board of flows, and week-by-week timelines drawn as thin coloured lines; the page runs 37 screens on desktop.

## Weak spots
- A 190px fixed header on desktop and a left sidebar leave little reading width on shorter screens.
- Captions and timeline labels are about 11px grey on pale grey and are hard to read.
