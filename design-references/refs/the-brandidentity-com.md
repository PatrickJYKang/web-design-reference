## Read
- The feed is one centred column on #f5f5f5, not a grid: each story is a single image 910px wide with its caption beneath, and the next starts about 200px lower. That is why the page runs 24.5 screens. Only the store section goes to four 320px tiles with 34px gaps.
- Two families swap roles by job. NB International sets the lead title, uppercase labels at 20 to 26px and section heads at 40 to 68px; Times Now sets captions and standfirsts at 32px/1.16, the commonest text at 44%, and one 70px interview headline. Nothing is heavier than weight 400.
- Captions are run-in: a small uppercase kind label, then the 32px serif title on the same line, held to a 674px measure and indented about 120px inside the image's left edge.
- The 83px sticky nav has no ground. Images blur and fade as they pass under it, and the full wordmark at its centre shortens to a four-character monogram once the page has moved.

## Weak spots
- In the capture the lead image is still a pink placeholder with a spinner and the jobs promo's media panel is a blank dark block, so the first two screens have no pictures.
- A white promo panel with 68px capitals sits between the lead story and the feed and reads as an advert for the site's own jobs board.
