## Read
- The scale is nearly flat: 16px and 14px serif (Arizona) for prose, and 12px and 10px uppercase mono (Departure Mono) for labels. The largest text on the page is the 16px intro line; the title is a mono wordmark drawn in blue, larger than any of these.
- Illustration does the work that headlines usually do. Each band is 1000 to 1400px tall and holds one large isometric or cutaway line drawing (floppy disk, CRT, hard drive) in pale blue, with mono callout labels and leader lines around it.
- Prose is pushed to narrow columns at the edges: content is 576px wide in the middle bands, and body paragraphs run in 3 columns of about 200px beside the drawings, like margin notes in a manual. The ground is #fbfbfb with 1px hairlines at 10% and 50% black.
- The call to action is quiet: a boxed "Early Access ($99)" button in mono, and a contents list of 8 chapters with word counts and dotted leaders.

## Weak spots
- Body text at 14px in about 200px columns with black at 50% alpha for the small mono labels is hard to read.
- Drawings are cropped by the 1440 frame at the top and bottom of several screens, so some labels lose their lines.
