## Read
- Three faces share the page: Poly Sans Median for the centred 96px w400 headline, Aeonik 16px w500 for UI and body (46% of text), and Studio Feixen for the italic-leaning display lines on the sector pills. All weights stay at w400 or w500.
- Cream #f0f1e3 is the ground and mint #abf7a1 is the only fixed accent (the "Let's Talk" button, a 900px band); yellow blobs behind the hero and pastel sector pills (sky blue, pink, mint, about 88px tall) bring the rest of the colour.
- Work is a three-column masonry, 437px wide with a 32px gap and 24px radius, in a band 2657px tall; each tile is a site mockup in its own colour, so the client work supplies the palette.
- The nav is a 108px fixed floating pill group at the top right (Work, Sectors, Let's Talk) and the logo strip of 18 marks (155x65, 51px gap) sits directly under the headline.

## Weak spots
- Tiles of different heights and colours compete, and the 2657px band is a long scroll with no labels on the work.
- The display face on the sector pills is hard to read at small sizes.
