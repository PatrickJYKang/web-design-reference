## Read
- The navigation is a diagram: about ten labelled nodes joined by 1px lines on a #f5f6f2 ground, with the owner's name as the one orange node, and different outlines (triangle, square, circle, hexagon) for initiatives, research, artifacts and paths. The page is one screen on desktop and on the phone.
- Type is one grotesque at 13px w400 for 60% of the text, 11px w400 uppercase tracked 0.05em for the panel labels, in #111111 with #888888 for the rest; the greys carry the hierarchy, so nothing is bold.
- The text lives in a small white panel beside the graph (about 185px wide at 1440), with 1px dashed rules between rows, radii of 3 to 5px, and four collapsible rows marked "+" ("-" when open) under a short bio.
- Motion is 1000ms on height and transform, so rows open slowly, while opacity changes take 100ms to 200ms.

## Weak spots
- 11px and 13px text with lighter #888888 labels, and faded unvisited nodes, make the graph hard to read.
- Name and role are a single paragraph in the panel; the site's one headline is the graph itself, which gives no entry point to someone who does not click.
