- The ground is grey, not white: #d9d9d9 for the first 900px and the story bands, #f3f3f3 and #ffffff for alternating bands, and one #1d1d1b band. A bag drawn in 3D that tilts on the first screen is the only content, with no headline.

## Weak spots

- The hero offers no product name, price or button; the only way into the shop is the small "Shop" label at the top left.
