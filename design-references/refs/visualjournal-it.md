- type: Metro Sans 100%. h1 18/1.44 w400. Sizes in px: 18/1.44 w400 (commonest, 100%).
- colour: part light, part dark. On screen #ffffff 17%, about #030302 5%. Text #000000 100%. No borders.

## Read
- The page is 84 images and two links. Tiles are 691×467, two across with 20px gaps and 20px side margins, so the grid takes 1401 of 1440 and images cover 86% of the first screen.
- Nothing is captioned: no title, credit, date or tag under a tile. The only words on a tile are the ones in the photograph, and the mix of dark, pale and saturated images is left to make the rhythm.
- All type is one style, 18px/1.44 weight 400 in #000000. The 78px fixed white bar puts the site name at the left edge and its one link at the start of the second column, so the bar sits on the same two columns as the tiles.
- Tiles are square-cornered with a #f5f5f5 fill behind each image and no border or shadow; pale images blend into it.

## Weak spots
- With no caption or credit on the index, a visitor cannot tell whose work a tile shows without opening it.
- 84 tiles in one unbroken run make 23 screens with no grouping, filter or way to jump.
