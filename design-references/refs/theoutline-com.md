## Read
- Every section changes ground: navy #04284a, black, purple #68268c, plum #661f4f, red #881910, pink #ff294c, so the page reads as a run of separate posters instead of one feed.
- Section titles (VERY ONLINE, MUST READS) are 160px uppercase, centred, in coral #fb6754 or mint #afffcc, set so large that the story tiles overlap their lower edge.
- Stories are 293×440 portrait tiles in rows of three, 40px apart, with the headline laid over a tinted image in a serif; the lead block is a 1120×1152 navy panel inside a coral frame.
- Black 900px bands with a full-bleed cut-out photo or object break the colour runs, and the page sits on a loud abstract backdrop in the margins; 14.8 desktop screens.

## Weak spots
- White headline text sits on busy tinted photos with no scrim, so several titles on the sheet are hard to read (the green-on-green and purple-on-purple ones).
- The 30px type with line-height 2.67 is the commonest size and leaves loose, gappy paragraphs.
