## Read
- The roster is a mosaic: five 362px columns, 10px apart, running 2358px tall with portraits of different heights, so the page opens as a wall of photographs. The photos run past both screen edges.
- The wordmark is set in a 96px display cut at the top and again at the foot, and nearly all other text is 14px w500 captions of the form "artist x brand" under each photo; type is sparse and stays out of the picture's way.
- A second voice, Garamond Narrow, appears in a 43.2px italic statement laid over the mosaic, and the contrast with the grotesque makes it read as a signature; it covers photographs and its first words are hard to read.
- Chrome is a 41px fixed nav of three or four small links; the end is a #e8e8e8 grey footer in columns (artists, explore, connect), and the 900px band below is #201d1d.

## Weak spots
- The italic statement sits across busy photographs, so the text is hard to read at its start and end.
- Captions are 14px black on whatever colour sits behind them, and the nav floats over the photo wall with no ground.
