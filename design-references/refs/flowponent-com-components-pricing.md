## Read
- A browsing tool, built as one: a top bar about 64px tall with a search field about 580px wide, a 52px icon rail and a 183px category list on the left, and a three-column masonry that is the only part that scrolls.
- The thumbnails are the only light surfaces. Each sits in a #e7e5e4 frame with 8px corners and about 14px of padding, about 374px wide at its own height (roughly 230 to 290px), 12px from its neighbours on black.
- Sidebar rows are 14px w500 with a 10px count right-aligned; the current category is a full-width violet bar and group labels are 12px w700 uppercase at 0.1em.
- The page title is 24px w700 in violet over three lines of 16px text. It is smaller than most hero deks because the grid is the content.

## Weak spots
- Thumbnails are soft at 374px wide: plan names and prices inside them cannot be read, so every tile needs a click to judge.
- Tiles carry no caption, so free and paid items look alike in the grid.
