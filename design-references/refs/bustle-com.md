## Read
- The masthead and section heads are set at 116px in a red #db3336 serif (the commonest red use), so the title of each section is bigger than any story headline, which is 33.6px or less.
- Story size steps down in one block: a 492px lead with a 33.6px serif headline, a column of 11 small 504×84 text rows, and 216px thumbnails with 12.8px labels.
- Sections switch ground rather than using rules: white, a navy panel with four 01 to 04 vertical tabs, a #1a1a1a Originals band, and a pale #e2e2f3 band, which breaks a 27-screen page into distinct areas.
- Images are illustrated or collaged covers with red and pink fills, so the red accent comes from the pictures as well as the type.

## Weak spots
- 27 screens of stacked blocks with a different layout each time; the only structure is the section head, so scanning is hard.
- Ad units and a newsletter box sit between stories at full width and break the reading rhythm.
