## Read
- Two voices: a Caslon-style serif for the 58.7px statement and project names, and Sohne for 14.5px captions, labels and the news column. Colour is #000000 on #ffffff only, so the client work supplies all the colour.
- A 498px project tile is paired with a second beside it, each with a 1px black rule, a serif name and a one-line sans description under the image. Six tiles, gap 20, sit left of a 239px news column.
- The wordmark is huge on the first screen (about 250px wide, left of a 498px hero image) and shrinks to a small centred mark in the 50px fixed nav on scroll.
- A pinned stage takes 2959px, with project images in the main column and news items (date, 14px headline, thumbnail) running alongside it, so two lists scroll as one.

## Weak spots
- Dense mixed content in two columns, with captions at 14.5px, gives little reading order.
- The serif statement has a deep first-line indent and a stray tiny "About us" label at its end.
