## Read
- Plus Jakarta Sans in w500 at every size (64/1.1, 48, 40 down to 16px, tracking -0.02 to -0.03em), so size and colour carry the hierarchy.
- Headings are two-tone: the opening sentences sit at about 40% opacity and the last line at full #050b10 ("The world is accelerating... I help you look like the market leader"), so each statement ends on its point.
- The page opens on a dark green gradient hero (30% image) with the four-line statement left-aligned, then drops to #f9f9f9 with white cards at 12px radius (405x371 in threes, 48px gap).
- A process section runs three steps as 640x460 cards, with a tick-mark scroll indicator and "Step 2" labels in the left margin; work follows as five 1312x720 mockups with 16px radius, one per 720px band.
- Pill buttons throughout, 42px tall, translucent white on dark.

## Weak spots
- The faded first half of each heading is about 40% opacity, which is under 3:1 against #f9f9f9.
- Five full-width mockups at 720px each stretch the work section; the page is 18.1 screens on a phone.
