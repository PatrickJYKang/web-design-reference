## Read
- One grotesque, Aktiv, at weight 300 throughout: 75px for section titles, 46.5px for the statement and 22.5px (46% of the text) for list rows. Hierarchy is size and the white at 100% or 50% opacity, with no bold.
- The opening is a pinned 2700px stage: a wordmark in dark grey (#333333) cropped by both edges, with a dotted white globe passing over it and a 22px statement at lower left, where "push the limits" is in full white and the rest in 50% white.
- Proof is an accordion of three counted rows (18 Projects, 11 Awards, 18 Clients), each a large number at the left, a title at about 22px and an arrow, with 1px rules in white at 10%. Opening a row shows a strip of 333px thumbnails or a 3-by-3 award list.

## Weak spots
- Dark grey letters on black at the wordmark are low contrast, and the 10.5px nav labels are tiny.
- Pinned stages of 2700px to 3600px make long scroll for content that is mostly lists.
