## Read
- The whole site is one screen (1 screen on desktop and on the phone): a #f2f2f2 field of project images, with no sections, cards, borders or shadows.
- Images are scattered in two staggered rows of unequal sizes (about 110 to 260px tall at 1440, edge-cropped at the left and right) with gaps of about 5px, so the field looks hand-laid and implies more work off screen.
- Type is one family, Cassette: 14px as the commonest size (87%) and a 38px w700 line, set in #333333. The only caption on the first screen is a short line at top right, "design & direction made to move", with a down arrow, about 14px.
- Links keep the browser's default #0000ee, which covers 34% of the text and is the only colour in the type, so the interface adds none to the pictures.

## Weak spots
- Default link blue on #f2f2f2 is easy to mistake for an unstyled page and does not belong to the images' palette.
- With no labels on the tiles, nothing says which image is which project until it is opened.
