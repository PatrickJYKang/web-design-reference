## Read
- One centred stack repeats down 34 screens, numbered down from 43: a number, a light-italic kicker at about 53px, the subject's name in uppercase Folio Book, a line sketch in a dotted frame about 320px wide, the magazine's name at 53px, then three or four lines of 12px caps. A 1px black rule closes each entry.
- The name is set to fill the 907px column whatever its length, which is why the sizes run from 130px to 317px. Leading 0.8 and −0.06em tracking make the two lines touch, so each entry reads as one block of the same width.
- No photographs and no colour: #000000 on #ffffff, and the hand sketches are the only pictures at 7% of the first screen.
- The chrome is eight 36px outlined pills pinned to the viewport: one turned vertical at the top left for the index, and five topic filters stacked at the right edge above a black round button.

## Weak spots
- The 12px caps saying who each subject is sit under names twenty times their size and are easy to miss.
- At roughly one entry per screen the list cannot be scanned; finding a name means opening the index pill.
