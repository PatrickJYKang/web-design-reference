## Read
- Every default is kept on purpose: Times at 20px for 72% of the text, a 50px uppercase masthead, no radii, shadows or transitions, and one 1px black rule under the nav.
- Images are dithered to a single ink (blue, green or red) on the page colour, so a dozen unrelated photographs read as one set.
- The ground carries information: the #f0f8ff block behind the top of the page is a battery meter laid over the #fff5d1 paper, and the footer lists server stats in 14px mono.
- One lead story (335px of text beside a 1045px image), then a four-column grid of 335px cards 20px apart: image, 23px title, 20px dek, 14px mono date.

## Weak spots
- Card titles (23px) and deks (20px) are nearly the same size and weight, so the grid has little hierarchy.
- The battery meter is unexplained unless you read the one line under the masthead.
