## Read
- Light is the voice: Unica at w300 for every size, 22px for half the text and 40px at most, in #777576 grey with #17171c for the few emphasised lines.
- The top is an 810px full-bleed hero with a centred 40px line over a ring photograph; below it, 4 × 360px photo columns and 12 × 312px product tiles with 1px gaps give the page its rhythm.
- Collection links are a row of text-only entries with a count, such as "Shop 20% Off [64]", under each band, so the nav lives in the page, not in a header.
- Photography is all tabletop still life on blue-grey paper and tissue, which gives a set of unrelated rings a shared ground.
- The header is 86px, sticky, with no fill, and a thin sale strip above it.

## Weak spots
- Light-grey w300 text at 13 to 14px is low contrast, and the muted link rows near the hero are hard to read against the photographs.
- The FAQ block sits in the middle of a shop page and takes more than a screen before the brand story.
