## Read
- Each band is exactly 900px tall and 100% media: a product shot, a pair of UI mock-ups on dark red, and a three-by-three grid of desk photographs. The page is a sequence of full-screen slides, not a scroll of sections.
- Type is Helvetica Neue only: a 30px w700 title with -0.03em tracking at top left (about 30px) and 13px w400 captions for 73% of the text. A small credit list sits bottom left, with a slide counter bottom right.
- The ground is #000000 with 87% share, so each slide's colour comes entirely from the project (silver bottle on beige, deep red), which changes slide to slide.
- Motion is slow: 560 to 1300ms transitions on opacity and translate. The only radius is 12px, used once.

## Weak spots
- The measured fixed nav is 900px tall, which means a full-viewport overlay; the sheet is dimmed by it and the photos look washed out.
- The 13px credit lines are small and low in contrast over the photograph.
