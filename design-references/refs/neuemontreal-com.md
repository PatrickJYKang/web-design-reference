## Read
- The typeface is the only material: one family set from 19.2px to 240px at weights 300 to 800, display sizes on 0.8 to 1 leading with tracking down to −0.06em, so each band doubles as a specimen of a different weight.
- Grounds mark chapters: a 900px #d82f2f opening with a black 199px w800 wordmark and one small video tile, then #1a1a1a for 79% of the page, then #f0e7d4 cream. On the dark ground, colour comes from single words set in green, yellow, blue and cream and from rotated poster cards scattered round a heading.
- Four pinned stages (900, 2988, 765 and 3670px) change their content in place while the page scrolls; one steps a centred heading through the weights with the weight's name as a small label above it. That is how the page runs to 25 screens.
- Chrome is three pills floating over every ground: a logo pill at left, a centred light pill of seven links about 590×40, and an outlined call-to-action pill at right.

## Weak spots
- Running text is set at 24px on a line-height of 1 with −0.03em tracking; the three-column story block is specimen-tight and tiring past a few lines.
- About 8,300px of the scroll is spent inside pinned stages, so long stretches of scrolling change one heading or image at a time.
