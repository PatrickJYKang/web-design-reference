## Read
- Two columns centred as one 976px block: a 208px rail, a 96px gap, a 672px reading column. The rail lists the five sections at about 13px with the date and category under them and stays pinned for all 8.6 screens; the current section is full-strength with a dash, the others 70%.
- One family carries every level: 39.1/1.25 bold title, 30.1 bold section heads, 20.8/1.62 body (82% of the text) in #1a1919 on #eae8e3. A monospace cut marks everything that is not prose: nav, the rail label, the date, and the one-line subtitle under the title.
- The title block is only the title and that subtitle, flush left on the column, with the first paragraph about 40px below. No byline, image or rule.
- What interrupts: line drawings in the text colour (three circles, four button outlines, two 3×3 grids), none taller than about 160px, each with a 13px caption; and under each section head a one-line summary at 17.2px with a 2px rule on its left. The first screen is 3% image.

## Weak spots
- Rail and nav text is about 13px, and the rail's inactive items are 70% ink on the grey ground: hard to read beside 20.8px body text.
