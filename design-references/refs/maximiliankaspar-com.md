## Read
- Everything is lowercase, in two sizes: a 113px h1 at 1.02 line height in a display face for the self-introduction, and 14px for the rest, 77% of it in a mono variant. There is no middle size.
- The first screen is an empty 900px band with the 113px statement set at the bottom, over a faint ASCII-art figure that runs behind the whole page.
- The grid is four columns of 358px with 28px gaps, 3 to 4 rows per project, and each project opens with a row of facts: name, one-line brief, a list of roles and client, year. Under each image its filename is the caption (such as ".jpg"), so metadata is styled as part of the layout.
- Thin vertical hairlines mark the columns, and the nav is two words, "photos" and "about", at top right.

## Weak spots
- The ASCII figure sits behind the project text and reduces contrast on the 12px captions.
- File names as captions say nothing about the work.
