## Read
- The hero is a full-width photograph that takes 76% of the first screen; the title does not appear until the second. The picture sells the project, the text explains it.
- The title block is a three-column row under a 1px rule: a small meta column (date, source, read time), the 55px/1.07 title in the middle, and a tag list at the right. Colour is #f5f5f5 with black text, so the project images carry all the colour.
- Body copy is serif (Times Now) at 22px/1.3, 80% of the text. The lead paragraph runs about 900px wide, then the paragraphs narrow to about 670px (read off the sheet), with images at 910px or two side by side at about 675px each.
- A sticky 83px bar with a centred wordmark and three icons stays over the page, with no ground of its own; images and text slide under it.

## Weak spots
- The bar has no background, so body text shows through it as the page scrolls (visible in the sheet), and the top strip's close button sits over the logo row.
- Images that have not loaded show as flat lilac rectangles (#c6b6cb), which leaves a large blank block between paragraphs.
