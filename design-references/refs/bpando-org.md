## Read
- The page is two 720px halves edge to edge with a 1px #eeeeee line between and no outer margin. The left runs the latest articles one per row; the right stacks a 2×2 grid of 320×268 archive tiles, a #1d1d1d membership panel and a two-across grid of black logo marks. The halves move separately: the panel stays in view for three screens while articles pass on the left.
- Title and excerpt are the same 25.4px/1.2 weight 500 at −0.02em. The title is #000000, the excerpt #000000@40% and cut at four lines, so ink alone separates them and the grey makes up 66% of all text.
- Pictures sit on #000000@5% tiles 656px wide with the caption directly under, no card edge, date or author. Logo marks get the same treatment: black on white, a name in about 16px below, a hairline between rows.
- The one colour is the 52px #fa5e00 sign-up button, 6px radius, inside the dark panel. The nav is a single line of 12.7px links kept right in a thin fixed white strip.

## Weak spots
- 25px excerpts at 40% black come to about 2.8:1 on white, and each ends mid-sentence in an ellipsis.
- The membership panel takes a full screen of the right half before the logos and sits beside three articles in a row.
